Your wallpaper stays. Here’s how to get started.

01 · Find an app

Search by name or initials, or use the A–Z rail to jump through your apps.

02 · Keep favorites close

Long-press an app to add it to Favorites. Tap the Favorites heading to collapse or expand it.

03 · Make it yours

Hold an empty area for Widgets and Launcher settings. Your current wallpaper stays unchanged. For White or Black, choose Home wallpaper → No wallpaper, then Theme.
