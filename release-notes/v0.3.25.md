## What's fixed

- When Android delivers the Home action, Alpharail now dismisses its tracked settings panels and dialogs instead of leaving them above Home.
- Returning Home also clears search and immediately hides the held-letter popup.
- Dialog tracking now covers both wallpaper and solid-theme modes, with guarded wallpaper-overlay cleanup.

- This update does not claim to fix the stock-launcher card or gesture animation in XOS Recents. Android/XOS controls that system component; leave the stock launcher enabled.
