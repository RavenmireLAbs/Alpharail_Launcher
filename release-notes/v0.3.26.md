## What's new

- **Settings search:** open Search settings at the top of Launcher settings to find controls by name, such as clock, alphabet, or schedule.
- **App shortcuts:** app-provided long-press actions now have an ↗ marker. Available pinned shortcuts are queried alongside dynamic and static shortcuts, subject to Android permissions and app support.
- **New app indicator:** a subtle New label marks apps installed after this feature is initialized. It clears after a successful launch from Alpharail. Toggle it under Home & apps → New app indicator. Existing apps and ordinary updates are not all marked new; launches outside Alpharail are not tracked.
- Updated the README to reflect current features, settings organization, and the 14 Abstract wallpapers.

## Validation

Native build and APK signature verification passed. These features have not yet been tested on-device or mirrored in the browser preview.
