## What's changed

- New users get a one-page welcome guide covering app search, Favorites, and launcher settings. The guide scrolls on smaller screens.
- Fresh installs show Home over the existing Android wallpaper without replacing it. Existing users' appearance choices stay unchanged.
- The held-letter popup and selected alphabet highlight now use the wallpaper's primary color by default, with contrasting text. Choose wallpaper color or one of 30 custom colors under **Launcher settings → App list → Alphabet color**. The theme accent is used when wallpaper colors are unavailable.
- Apps whose names do not start with A–Z, such as 2FAS Auth, are grouped under **#** after the letter sections in both the app list and alphabet rail.
- Updated the browser welcome guide and added a 2FAS Auth example.
