## What's changed

- Long-pressing an empty area on Home now opens **Widgets** and **Launcher settings** in a centered menu.
- With wallpaper enabled, translucent panels show just the wallpaper behind them. App icons, labels, the clock, and the A–Z rail are hidden while a panel is open, and the app list keeps its scroll position when you close it.
