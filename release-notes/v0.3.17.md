## What's changed

- Find apps using their initials in quick search, with Enter to launch the best match.
- Save and apply wallpaper presets from Launcher settings.
- Collapse or expand Favorites by tapping its heading.
- Customize the Home clock: compact size, hide the date, or show the next alarm time.
- Control Chats per app: mute new-message dots or hide message previews.
- Removed the Wuxia & Xianxia wallpaper section and its ten bundled wallpapers. All 14 Abstract wallpapers and their Light/Dark sections remain unchanged.
- Updated the interactive browser preview to match.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/RavenmireLAbs/Alpharail_Launcher/compare/v0.3.16...v0.3.17
