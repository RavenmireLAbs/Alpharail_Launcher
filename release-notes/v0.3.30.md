## What's changed

- Redesigned the floating bubble panel with a conversation list, initials avatars, source-app labels, and rounded notification message bubbles.
- Tap a conversation to see available notification messages, a bottom reply composer when supported, and Open in original app.
- Replies submitted through the panel appear right-aligned while the panel remains open. Submitted does not mean delivered; Android/source apps control delivery.
- Expand or shrink the panel, collapse it, or dismiss the bubble until the next notification.
- Added **Chat bubble → Bubble appearance** with Follow Alpharail, Light, and Dark themes plus six accent colors.

## Privacy and limits

Bubble previews remain off by default. Hidden/locked apps and secret notifications remain excluded; overlay screenshot protection stays enabled. The bubble does not persist conversation history and cannot embed another messaging app, retrieve full history, or provide its attachment picker. Notification updates may close the panel. Source-app support and Android permissions determine available messages/actions.

Native build and signature verification passed; the redesigned interface still needs on-device testing. Browser preview is unchanged.
