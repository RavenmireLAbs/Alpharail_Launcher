## What's new

- Optional draggable **Chat bubble** under Notifications & Chats. Opens active messaging notifications, supported conversation actions, and quick replies. Requires notification access and Display over other apps. Previews default off; hidden/locked apps are excluded, and the overlay uses screenshot protection. The bubble does not store history.
- Chats search, Pinned/New/Earlier sections, expandable previews, and failed replies kept open for retry.
- Optional **six-hour notification history** with private, backup-excluded storage, available picture previews, attachment-type labels, links, and replies submitted through Alpharail. Turning retention off clears it. Retention is capped at 100 snapshots. Expired snapshots are filtered/purged on access or capture; Android may defer physical cleanup while the app is not running. Actual documents or photos absent from notifications cannot be retrieved.
- Drag handles to reorder pinned Favorites, retaining existing move actions.
- Duplicate personal-app search names show package/profile details.
- Home layout controls for clock/date and icon visibility, row spacing, margins, section spacing, and search-bar presentation.
- Independent 12/24-hour clock formats and date formats; Favorites-only names; alphabet backgrounds/text sizes; wallpaper overlays; Chats density and sender-photo controls.
- Cache notification-dot state and wallpaper colors; reduce unnecessary Home list refreshes.

## Notes

These native changes need on-device testing and are not yet mirrored in the browser preview. Bubble availability and actions depend on Android permissions, battery restrictions, and source-app support. This release does not claim to fix system Recents behavior.
