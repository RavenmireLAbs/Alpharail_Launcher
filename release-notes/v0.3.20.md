## What's changed

- **Alphabet color** now displays a visual grid of all 30 color swatches instead of a name-only list.
- Each swatch includes its color name, with a contrasting checkmark and border indicating the selected color.
- **Wallpaper major color** remains available above the palette, and the picker scrolls on smaller screens.
